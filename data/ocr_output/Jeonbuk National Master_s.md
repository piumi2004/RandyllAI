## Important Notes for Application

- The application will be completed only after all required information has been entered and the application fee has been paid by the application deadline.
- Applicants must carefully check their eligibility and the department to which they are applying before submitting the application. The application fee is non-refundable after the application deadline.
- Thd   n  p     od .
- In the case of multiple applications within the same application round, applications other than the initial application will be canceled.
- Applicants must enter all information accurately, including email address and phone number.
- An application number will be issued after submission and will be used for information regarding the interview schedule, admission results.
- Even if you have completed the online application, if you do not submit the required documents until submission period, you will not be able to take the interview and will be disqualified.
- Applicants should submit completed paperwork during application period, if applicants fail to submit the required documents which affects admission status, he/she will be disqualified without further notice.
- Only language proficiency test scores issued within the past two (2) years will be accepted. (TOPIK, TOEFL, IELTS, TEPS, and New TEPS; JLPT excluded)
- If applicants submit the scanned copies of documents, they must submit original documents later. If these two documents are not the same, your admission cannot be guaranteed.
- All the submitted documents except the original diploma, graduation certificate will not be returned.
- Applicants who submitted a certificate of expected graduation at the time of application must submit the original degree certificate or graduation certificate to the Office of International Affairs by February 19, 2027. Failure to do so will result in cancellation of admission.

- ① Admitted candidates staying in Korea must change or extend their visa through the Office of International Affairs after paying tuition and before their current period of stay expires
- ② Admitted candidates staying overseas who need to obtain a student visa (D-2) must apply for the visa at the Korean Embassy or Consulate in their home country. Please note that the passport must be valid for at least two (2) years. - ③ JBNU will post an admission letter to admitted candidates staying overseas and must apply for visa at the Korean Consulate or Embassy by themselves.
- ④ Admitted candidates must check the required document for the Students visa on the website of the Korean embassy in your country because documents are different each Country.
