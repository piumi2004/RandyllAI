6.  Principles  for  Selection  Based  on  Practical  Skills
a.  Selection  Method:  Selection  will  be  made  in  order  of  highest  scores  based  on  total  score  ranking.
B.  Tie-breaking  Criteria:  In  the  event  of  a  tie,  the  ranking  will  be  determined  according  to  the  criteria  below.
[General  Practical  Skills  Track,  Equity  Opportunity  Track  (Outside  Quota),  Rural  Student  Track  (Outside  Quota)]
-  1st  Priority:  Highest  scores  in  the  practical  skills  test  -
2nd  Priority:  Highest  scores  in  the  interview  test  -  3rd
Priority:  Highest  scores  in  student  academic  transcript  grades  (converted  scores)
ÿ  Other  matters  shall  be  determined  by  the  Admissions  Committee.
