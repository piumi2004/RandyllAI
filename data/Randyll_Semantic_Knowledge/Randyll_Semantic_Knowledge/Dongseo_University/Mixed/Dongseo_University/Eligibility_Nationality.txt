04 Eligibility & Requirements
Classification
Eligibility and Requirements
· Parents and applicant must have citizenship from a country other than Korea.
Nationality-related Notes
※
· Applicants with dual nationality or stateless status are not eligible to apply.
· If the applicant and their parents have renounced or lost Korean nationality,
official documentation proving the loss of nationality (including details and
the exact date of loss/renunciation) must be submitted.
· Receipts or reports of nationality renunciation/loss that only confirm the
submission of the application will not be accepted.
· Applicant must have completed secondary school (or be expected to complete
secondary school before admission) either in or outside Korea.
