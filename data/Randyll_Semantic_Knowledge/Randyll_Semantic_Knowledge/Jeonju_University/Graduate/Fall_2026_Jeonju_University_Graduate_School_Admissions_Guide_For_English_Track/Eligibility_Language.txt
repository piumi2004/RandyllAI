3. Eligibility Requirements
Master's
Persons who have obtained or are expected to obtain (by August 2026) a bachelor's degree from a domestic or
foreign accredited university
Academic Qualifications
Persons recognized by the Minister of Education as having equivalent academic qualifications
Nationality
Both the applicant and both parents must be foreign nationals
Language Proficiency Requirements
Applicants for English-language track departments must submit a valid score of TOEFL iBT 59, IELTS 5.5, or New TEPS 202 or higher within the
Applicants whose native language is English, or who hold nationality of a country where English is a legally recognized official language, are exempt
from submitting a language proficiency score.
