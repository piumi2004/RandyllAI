LOCATION: heading: 2. Matriculation Procedures after Admission / passage 1

- ① Admitted candidates staying in Korea must change or extend their visa through the Office of International Affairs after paying tuition and before their current period of stay expires
- ② Admitted candidates staying overseas who need to obtain a student visa (D-2) must apply for the visa at the Korean Embassy or Consulate in their home country. Please note that the passport must be valid for at least two (2) years. Admitted candidates who obtain a new passport must immediately inform the Office of International Affairs of their new passport number.
- ③ JBNU will post an admission letter to admitted candidates staying overseas and must apply for visa at the Korean Consulate or Embassy by themselves.
- ④ Admitted candidates must check the required document for the Students visa on the website of the Korean embassy in your country because documents are different each Country.
