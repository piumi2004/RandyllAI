3. National Health Insurance
※ All International students must subscribe to National Health Insurance after entry
1) Enrollment Time : After Alien registration process (Automatically subscribed)
- Subscription is not allowed until alien registration is completed. Therefore, It is recommended to subscribe
extra insurance for safety reasons or in cases where the hospital is likely to be used before registration
2) Monthly fee : 76,390KRW
- The above amount is based on the amount paid in 2026 and maybe increased in the future.
- International students are given a partial reduction from the total amount paid
- Original Monthly fee : 152,780KRW
Reduction
50% Reduction(Pay 76,390KRW)
3) Payment Method : Bill will be sent to your home, pay fee after confirming the amount and account
