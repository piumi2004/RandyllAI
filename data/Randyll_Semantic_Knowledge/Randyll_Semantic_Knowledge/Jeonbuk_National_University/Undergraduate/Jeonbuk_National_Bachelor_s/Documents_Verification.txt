- Since July 14, 2007, the Republic of Korea has been part of the 1961 Hague Convention abolishing the Requirement
of Legalization for Foreign Public Documents. The Convention provides for the simplified certification or public
(including notarized) documents to be used in countries that have joined the convention.
- An Apostille ensures that public documents issued in one signatory country will be recognized as valid in
another signatory country.
