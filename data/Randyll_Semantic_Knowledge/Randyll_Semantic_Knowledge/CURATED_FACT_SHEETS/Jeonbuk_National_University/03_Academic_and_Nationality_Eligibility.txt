University: Jeonbuk National University
Topic: 03 Academic and Nationality Eligibility
Source: Jeonbuk National Master_s.md, Eligibility for Application
Source status: university admission guide / OCR transcription where specified

Freshman master's applicants need a bachelor's degree or equivalent; doctoral applicants a master's degree or equivalent. Expected graduates must obtain their degree before first semester day. Nationality path 1: applicant and both parents do not have Korean citizenship. Alternate path 2: non-Korean applicant completed elementary through undergraduate education outside Korea. Transfer requires separate credits/semester conditions by department.
