Document screening 40 points; interview 60 points (major knowledge/potential 30, intellectual attitude 10, research plan 10, language proficiency 10). Interviews in Korean or English, online for overseas applicants. Applicants below 60 total points, absent from interview, or with inadequate/false documents may be rejected.
