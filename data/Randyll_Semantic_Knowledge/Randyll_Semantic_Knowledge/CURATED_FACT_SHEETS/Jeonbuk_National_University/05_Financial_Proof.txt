Topic: 05 Financial Proof
Source: Jeonbuk National Master_s.md, Timeline and Required Documents

Original bank statement KRW 16,000,000 or more (source approximates USD 13,000). Korean residents: successful applicants use Korean bank account in own name, issued within 30 days before visa application. Overseas: applicant or parent's overseas account; issue within 30 days of submission; submit before visa application if not supplied earlier. Possible alternatives include eligible financial sponsorship or JBNU scholarship certificate.
