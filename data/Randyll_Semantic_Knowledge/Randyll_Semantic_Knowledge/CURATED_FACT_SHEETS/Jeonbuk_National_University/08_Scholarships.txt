Freshman full-time students: up to 80% tuition reduction first semester, amount and beneficiaries subject to change. Continuing academic scholarship approximately 15–100% waiver selected by department for GPA 3.63+; TOPIK scholarship 15% reduction at level 4+; research scholarship 50% for selected full-time students; BK research scholarship may provide monthly amount to eligible participating students.
