Guide specifies printed application receipt, admission form, research plan, personal data agreement, relevant degree/graduation certification, academic transcripts, graduation verification, applicable language score, proof of nationality/family relationship, proof of funds, passport, applicable ARC and KRW 80,000 application fee. Masters/doctoral/transfer checklists differ; consult original checklist for which items are mandatory.
