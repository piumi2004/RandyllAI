Topic: 13 Health Insurance
Source: Dongseo University.pdf, p.17

International students in Korea must enroll in National Health Insurance after foreign resident registration; monthly premium applies. Private insurance is required on arrival before registration. Lack of insurance may affect scholarship benefits.
