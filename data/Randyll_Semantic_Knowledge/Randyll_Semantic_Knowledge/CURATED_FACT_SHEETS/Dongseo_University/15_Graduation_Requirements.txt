Korean-track undergraduates: TOPIK 4 for graduation; arts/PE undergraduates: TOPIK 3; English-track undergraduates: TOEFL iBT 3.5 (previous 59), IELTS 5.5, CEFR B2 or NEW TEPS 202. Freshmen require 8 semesters; third-year transfers 4 semesters; fourth-year transfers 2 semesters. Graduate department-specific credit and graduation rules must be confirmed directly.
