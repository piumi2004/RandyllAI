University: Dongseo University
Topic: 04 Academic Eligibility
Source: Dongseo University.pdf, p.8
Source status: university admission guide / OCR transcription where specified

Undergraduate freshman: completed or expected to complete secondary school before admission. Sophomore transfer: at least one year/two semesters and at least 25% bachelor's credits. Junior transfer: at least two years/four semesters and 50% credits, or graduation from a two-/three-year college. Senior transfer: graduation from a three-year college. Master's: bachelor's degree completed or expected. Doctoral: master's degree completed or expected. GED, home schooling, cyber school, adult education not recognized.
