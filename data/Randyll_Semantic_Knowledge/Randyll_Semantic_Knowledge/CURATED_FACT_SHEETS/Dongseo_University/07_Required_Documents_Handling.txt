Source: Dongseo University.pdf, pp.9-11

Required documents are detailed in the original guide pp.9-11 and differ by applicant type. Documents not in Korean or English must be translated and notarized. Graduating students must provide final academic transcripts and graduation certificates before admission. Notarized documents and certificates must be issued within one year of application. Originals required; submitted documents are generally not returned. Consult the original checklist for exact item-by-item requirements.
