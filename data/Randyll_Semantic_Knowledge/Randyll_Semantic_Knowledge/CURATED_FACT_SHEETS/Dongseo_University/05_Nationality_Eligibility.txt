University: Dongseo University
Topic: 05 Nationality Eligibility
Source: Dongseo University.pdf, p.8
Source status: university admission guide / OCR transcription where specified

Applicant and parents must be citizens of countries other than South Korea. Dual nationals and stateless applicants are not eligible under the guide. Previous Korean nationality loss requires supporting official proof, including dates.
