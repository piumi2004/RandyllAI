Source: Dongseo University.pdf, p.17

Applicants abroad must apply for a visa at a Korean embassy/consulate. D-4 holders must change to D-2 before semester starts; D-10 holders change status in person at immigration office. Visa documents are issued individually. Financial documentation and graduation certificate may be required. The university does not issue student visas itself.
