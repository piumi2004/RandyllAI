Korean track: TOPIK level 3 or above, or Korea Immigration and Integration Program level 3 / pre-evaluation score at least 61, or King Sejong Institute Intermediate 1 or above. English track: TOEFL iBT 3.5 (previous scale 59), IELTS 5.5, CEFR B2, NEW TEPS 202 or higher. Scores must be valid at application. Certain qualifying English-speaking-country applicants may be exempt with evidence of education in English.
