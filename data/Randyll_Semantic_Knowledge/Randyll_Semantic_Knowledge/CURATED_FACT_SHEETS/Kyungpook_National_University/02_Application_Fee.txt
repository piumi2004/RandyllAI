KRW 80,000 application fee per admission track; paid through online application. Unpaid applications automatically canceled. Application number issued after payment. Guide warns applications and fees nonrefundable after payment, but earlier section also lists full refunds for withdrawals before application deadline; verify this internal inconsistency directly with KNU.
