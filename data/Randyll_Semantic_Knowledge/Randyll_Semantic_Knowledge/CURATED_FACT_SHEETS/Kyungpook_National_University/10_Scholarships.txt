First-semester TOPIK 3 waives Tuition Fee 1 (20% overall); TOPIK 4 waives 50% of Tuition Fee 2 (40% overall); TOPIK 5 waives all of Tuition Fee 2 (80% overall). Additional KNU language-program, dormitory, continuing TOPIK and academic excellence schemes apply subject to conditions. University budget may limit dormitory scholarship.
