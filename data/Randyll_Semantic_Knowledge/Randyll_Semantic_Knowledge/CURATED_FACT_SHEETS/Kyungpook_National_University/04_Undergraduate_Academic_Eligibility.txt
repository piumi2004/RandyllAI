Topic: 04 Undergraduate Academic Eligibility
Source: Kyungpook National University Undergraduate.md, Eligibility
Source status: university admission guide / OCR transcription where specified

Freshmen need school education equivalent to Korean elementary, middle and high school, subject to country-specific duration rules. GED, homeschooling, online/adult education excluded. Third-year transfer: completed accredited 2-/3-year college, or at least two years/four semesters of 4-year university with over 65 credits. Transfers limited to identical/similar academic major; other exclusions apply.
