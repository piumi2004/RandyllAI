Post-interview financial proof: bank balance in applicant/parent name; those in Korea with ARC need Korean bank statement in own name. Parent account requires their employment/business evidence. Bank certificates issued within 30 days. Scholarships below KRW 16,000,000 require additional documentation to cover shortfall. Exact baseline certificate amount is partially corrupted in OCR and should be checked against original.
