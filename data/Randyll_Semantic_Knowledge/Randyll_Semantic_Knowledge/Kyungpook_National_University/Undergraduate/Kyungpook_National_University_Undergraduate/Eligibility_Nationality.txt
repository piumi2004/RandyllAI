SECTION: &lt;Nationality Requirement for All Applicants&gt;
LOCATION: heading: &lt;Nationality Requirement for All Applicants&gt; / passage 1

- Applicants and both of their parents must be non-Korean nationals.
- Nationality criterion is evidenced only if applicants and both of their parents acquire non-Korean nationality by the time the applicant begins the curriculum corresponding to the educational programs of Korean high schools.
- Multi-nationals with Korean citizenship are not eligible.
- Eligible Nationality Status must be established before the application due date.
