LOCATION: heading: 1. Application Documents (After Online Application) / passage 4

- Important Notes on Required Documents
- A. All required documents must be prepared in Korean or English. If a document is written in another language,
- a notarized translation must be submitted.
- B. When uploading application documents, prepare and upload one PDF file for each item (① – ④) listed above.
- Do NOT combine all documents into a single PDF file.
- C. Passport: Submit a copy of a valid passport that has not expired. Applicants must notify the Office of International Affairs of any changes to their passport information.
- Applicants holding Uzbekistani citizenship must submit an international passport.
- D. Applicants Graduating from High Schools in China
- General High Schools: Submit notarized translations of the graduation certificate and transcript, along with the English version of the CHSI (学信网) verification certificate.
- Vocational High Schools: Submit the following ①, ②&amp; ③
- ① School Information Verification Form (Form 3 on page 18)
- ②Notarized translations of the graduation certificate and transcript
- ③ One of the following (a or b):
- a. Graduation certificate issued by the school itself: Submit a document verified by the Provincial Education Department
- (or Municipal Education Bureau) and authenticated by Apostille.
- b. Graduation certificate issued by the local education authority: Submit a document authenticated by Apostille.
- E. Documents Verifying Parents' Nationality and Family Relationship
- Copies of identification documents of the applicant and both parents
- Official document(s) showing the family relationship between the applicant and parents and the nationality of each person
- Only documents notarized within six months prior to the date of submission will be accepted.
