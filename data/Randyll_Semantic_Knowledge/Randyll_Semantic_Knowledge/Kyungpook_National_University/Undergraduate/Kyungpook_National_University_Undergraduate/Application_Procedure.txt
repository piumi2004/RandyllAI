--- RECORD 2 ---
SECTION: 1. Applications
LOCATION: heading: 1. Applications / passage 2

- Applicants may apply to both Track I and Track II simultaneously.
- A. Application Period
- Spring Semester: Oct. 12, 2026 (Mon), 09:00 – Oct. 30, 2026 (Fri), 18:00
- Fall Semester: Apr. 12, 2027 (Mon), 09:00 – Apr. 30, 2027 (Fri), 18:00
- B. Application Website: Uway Apply (Link available on the KNU OIA website: www.uwayapply.com)
- C. Application Number: Automatically generated upon successful completion of the online application
- D. Application Procedure:
- During the application period, upload the required documents listed in "6. Required Documents" (Page 11).
- Upload one PDF file for each document item (① – ④).
- Do NOT upload all required documents as a single PDF file


--- RECORD 3 ---
SECTION: Online Application Process
LOCATION: heading: Online Application Process / passage 1

- Upload the required documents (1) in the order listed under "6. Required Documents" and (2) as one PDF file per document item.
- ▶Applications applying to both Track I and Track Il must upload a separate set of application documents for each application number.
- ▶An application is considered complete only after the application fee has been paid. Once payment has been made, no changes may be made to the application.
- ▶The application fee will be fully refunded if the application is withdrawn before 18:00 on Oct. 30, 2026 (Spring Semester) or before 18:00 on Apr. 30, 2027 (Fall Semester). No refund will be provided after the application deadline.
- ▶Applicants should keep their five-digit application number for future reference.
- ▶Heavy website traffic is expected on the application deadline. Applicants are strongly advised to complete their application well in advance.
- ▶For inquiries regarding the online application system: Uway Apply (+82-1588-8988)
