Since July 14, 2007, the Republic of Korea has been part of the 1961 Hague Convention abolishing the Requirement of Legalization for Foreign Public Documents. The Convention provides for the use of simplified certification of public documents (including notarized documents) among countries that have joined the convention. The "Apostille" designation ensures that public documents issued in one signatory country will be recognized as valid in another signatory country.
