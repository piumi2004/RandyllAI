17.  Violations  of  college  admission  application
A.  Caution  regarding  violations  of  multiple  applications  and  double  registration
ÿ  Relevant  Basis:  Article  42  and  Articles  42-2,  3  of  the  Enforcement  Decree  of  the  Higher  Education  Act
ÿ  Admission  will  be  cancelled  if  the  prohibition  on  multiple  applications  and  double  registration  is  violated.
ÿ  Scope  of  Permitted  Multiple  Applications:  For  universities  accepting  rolling  admissions  (including  industrial  universities,  teacher  training  colleges,  and  junior  colleges),  multiple  applications  are  permitted  even  if  the  admission  periods  are  the  same.
