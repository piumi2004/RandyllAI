** King Sejong Institute Middle Level 1 or higher or Social Integration Program of Ministry of Justice Level 3 or higher or pre-evaluation 61 point or higher

   it is paid within the scope of the tuition)
2. All the scholarships apply from the second semester
3. All the scholarships are only applicable for students who complete courses of over 12 credits in the previous semester(4th year : over 9 credits)
4. All the scholarships are awarded to the students as below
  - Freshman : up to 8 semesters / Transfer(2nd year) : up to 6 semesters / Transfer(3rd year) : up to 4 semesters

   subject to change according to the changes in Gachon University’s Scholarship Policy.
