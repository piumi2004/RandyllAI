RANDYLL ATOMIC KNOWLEDGE EXTRACTION

These are new text files reconstructed from text passages, NOT sliced PDFs. A single original PDF page may contribute information to different topic files. Each passage retains its source document and page or OCR section anchor.

OCR documents (Jeonbuk Graduate, Kyungpook Undergraduate) were processed at markdown section/paragraph/table row level. For other PDFs, content is classified from smaller text passages, not whole pages. This is an automated, first-pass extraction; it is NOT a manually fact-checked rewrite. Complex multi-column PDF tables may have imperfect reading order. OCR errors and incomplete data remain possible; verify tuition, deadlines, eligibility and visa decisions against originals.

Different university guides may cover multiple degree levels. Degree shown as Mixed when not reliably clear from filename. Use source identifiers to distinguish guides. Do not assume information is current beyond guide publication.
